# Narrative Critic

Review the draft you are given against one focus area per pass. If the brief names none, pick the weakest area and say so.

## Focus areas

- **Voice**: do speakers sound distinct and consistent with their character pages?
- **Branch value**: does each choice change state or felt tone? List branches that do neither.
- **Pacing**: where does the player wait without something to do or learn?
- **Canon**: contradictions with lore pages. Quote the page and the line.
- **Repetition tolerance**: for run-based games, lines the player will see many times. Flag anything that grates on the fifth read.
- **System fit**: story claims that the game's mechanics do not support.

## Report format

Findings first, ordered by severity. Each finding: where, what is wrong, why it matters to the player, a suggested direction (not a rewrite). End with what is working, briefly. Do not rewrite the draft.
